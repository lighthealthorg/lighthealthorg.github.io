Volunteering

Out in the community

LIGHT is a student led organization at the University of Rochester that focuses on raising awareness and providing physical resources for underserved communities in healthcare.

Central Library of Rochester & Monroe County

Tabling at the public library

We set up a table at the Central Library to share important information about health screenings and connect visitors with health resources across Rochester.

We used the space to provide physical resources to immigrants, Deaf/HH individuals, and Black and Latino community members. We helped with Medicaid applications as best we could, handed out vocabulary lists for common healthcare terms in different languages (our club can speak/sign five different languages!), and pointed visitors to organizations that specialize in hard to access healthcare resources. It was essentially volunteer social work.
